# Risk Metrics Evaluation Table

This matrix outlines project evaluation metrics, measurement baselines, and risk thresholds with corrected English grammar and professional terminology.

| Metric | Description | Measurement Baseline | No Risk | Low Risk | Medium Risk | High Risk | Very High / Prohibitive |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Effort** | Effort is calculated using story points, representing the total person-hours of all assigned team members. | 100% = Total project man-hours – hours already completed | 0% | 0% – 25% | 25% – 50% | 50% – 75% | > 75% (Prohibitive) |
| **Budget** | Total remaining budget. | 100% = Total remaining budget – profit | 0% | 0% – 10% | 10% – 25% | 25% – 30% | 30% – 40% (Very High)<br>> 40% (Prohibitive) |
| **Time** | Remaining time, or the total number of sprints required if this change request is added to the schedule. | 100% = Total time left or total sprints needed to complete | 0% | 0% – 10% | 10% – 25% | 25% – 30% | 30% – 40% (Very High)<br>> 60% (Prohibitive) |
| **Expertise** | Measured by evaluating the technical stack and skill set of each team member. | 100% = All team members | 0% | 0% – 20% | 20% – 30% | 30% – 60% | 60% – 80% (Very High)<br>> 80% (Prohibitive) |
| **Resource Allocation** | Assesses existing company resources versus additional resources that need to be acquired. | 100% = Remaining budget allocated for resources – profit | 0% | 0% – 10% | 10% – 25% | 25% – 30% | 30% – 40% (Very High)<br>> 40% (Prohibitive) |
| **Legal Compliance** | Verifies whether the proposal or change complies with legal regulations. | Is it legal? (Yes / No) | Yes | N/A | N/A | N/A | No (Prohibitive) |
